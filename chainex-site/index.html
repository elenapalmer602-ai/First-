How we get paid

We only get paid when you save.

How we're paid depends on what your brand needs, and we always agree the arrangement that works best for your situation, in writing, before any work starts. When the job is getting you the same product for less, it's simple: we take a share of the saving and you keep the rest.

  • No saving, no fee. If we can't beat the price you pay today, you don't pay us a thing.
  • You keep 80% of every saving. Our fee is 20% of the difference, agreed up front and never hidden in your unit price.
  • Your brand, treated like ours. The more you save, the more we earn, so we push for the best price, quality and terms as if the brand were our own.

When you save, we earn. When you don't, we don't. That's why we treat every brand like our own.

Quality and verification

Proof, not promises.

Every supplier is checked before money moves, and every product is tested before it reaches your customers. You get the evidence in writing.

01

Before money moves

Supplier verification

Anyone can send a quotation. We confirm the company behind it is real, active and safe to pay.

  • ISO 22716 GMP
  • CPSR
  • UK SCPN
  • EU CPNP
  • US MoCRA

Examples of the standards and registrations we check, depending on your product and market.

  • Legal entity and registration checked against official records
  • Certificates confirmed with the issuing body where possible
  • Payment safety: the invoicing company, shipper and bank account name must match
  • The real manufacturer identified, not just the trading company
  • Market compliance documents collected, with any gaps listed
02

Before it reaches your customers

Sample testing, documented

Samples reach you and us at the same time.

When a supplier's samples are ready, we receive one set and a matching set is sent to you at the same time. You hold the product and judge it for yourself, while we put ours through a documented test.

Then we send you the file, so you can decide with your own impression and our evidence side by side.

What goes into your test file

The same checks for every sample, so the comparison is fair.

  1. 01
    Logged on arrival

    Every sample is photographed as it arrives. Lot numbers, manufacture and expiry dates are recorded, and any transit damage is noted.

    In your fileArrival photos and batch log

  2. 02
    Packaging and components

    Outer carton, unit box, label print, seals, closures, applicator and fill level, inspected and measured across several units.

    In your fileComponent checklist with measurements

  3. 03
    Against your current product

    We order your existing product as a customer would, then test the new sample next to it. Same light, same checks, same scoring, so you can see which is better.

    In your fileSide by side scores

  4. 04
    Against the other suppliers

    Samples of the same product from each shortlisted supplier go on the same bench and the same score sheet, compared side by side.

    In your fileSupplier ranking

  5. 05
    Against your customer complaints

    We take the recurring complaints from your reviews, turn each one into a test, and show whether the new sample fixes it.

    In your fileComplaint by complaint results

  6. 06
    Documented on camera

    Every check is photographed and filmed under the same controlled light, so you see what we saw, not just our opinion.

    In your filePhotos and video for every check

How your product reaches customers changes your costs, delivery times and complaint rate. We cost each route on real quotes and recommend the one that fits your stage, not the biggest one.

Questions

The things brands ask us first.

Anything else, just ask. We would rather answer it now than after you have committed.

Do you manufacture products?

No. We are a sourcing and supply chain partner. We find, verify and manage the manufacturers who make your product, and we act on your side throughout.

How are you paid?

Arrangements are tailored to each engagement and set out in writing before you commit to anything. When we are getting you the same product for less, our fee is 20% of the saving, so we only earn when you save. Nothing is hidden in your unit cost, and there are no long lock ins. See how it works

Can you work with my current supplier?

Yes. We can benchmark your current supplier against alternatives. Sometimes the best outcome is fixing what you already have, and if that is what the evidence shows, we will tell you.

I only dropship right now. Is that a problem?

Not at all. It is a common starting point. We can improve your current setup first, then model when moving to bulk stock, or to your own branded product, makes financial sense.

Is my brand kept confidential?

Yes. We do not share your brand or business details with suppliers without your permission, and we are happy to sign a non disclosure agreement before you share anything sensitive.

Which products and markets do you cover?

Our focus is beauty, skincare and personal care, where quality and compliance matter most. We support brands selling into the UK, EU and US, and map the compliance and duty position for each market.

What does the first conversation cost?

Nothing. We look at your product, your costs and your current issues, and tell you honestly whether we can improve on them. If we cannot, we will say so.
