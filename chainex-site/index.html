Quality and verification

Proof, not promises.

Every supplier is checked before money moves, and every product is tested before it reaches your customers. You get the evidence in writing.

Supplier verification

Anyone can send a quotation. We confirm the company behind it is real, active and safe to pay.

  • Legal entity and registration checked against official records
  • Certificates confirmed with the issuing body where possible
  • Payment safety: the invoicing company, shipper and bank account name must match
  • The real manufacturer identified, not just the trading company
  • Market compliance documents collected, with any gaps listed
  • ISO 22716 GMP
  • CPSR
  • UK SCPN
  • EU CPNP
  • US MoCRA

Questions

The things brands ask us first.

Anything else, just ask. We would rather answer it now than after you have committed.

Do you manufacture products?

No. We are a sourcing and supply chain partner. We find, verify and manage the manufacturers who make your product, and we act on your side throughout.

How are you paid?

Arrangements are tailored to each engagement and set out in writing before you commit to anything. Fees are built around measurable outcomes, such as verified savings against your current costs. Nothing is hidden in your unit cost, and there are no long lock ins.

Can you work with my current supplier?

Yes. We can benchmark your current supplier against alternatives. Sometimes the best outcome is fixing what you already have, and if that is what the evidence shows, we will tell you.

I only dropship right now. Is that a problem?

Not at all. It is a common starting point. We can improve your current setup first, then model when moving to bulk stock, or to your own branded product, makes financial sense.

Is my brand kept confidential?

Yes. We do not share your brand or business details with suppliers without your permission, and we are happy to sign a non disclosure agreement before you share anything sensitive.

Which products and markets do you cover?

Our focus is beauty, skincare and personal care, where quality and compliance matter most. We support brands selling into the UK, EU and US, and map the compliance and duty position for each market.

What does the first conversation cost?

Nothing. We look at your product, your costs and your current issues, and tell you honestly whether we can improve on them. If we cannot, we will say so.
