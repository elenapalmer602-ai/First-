Verification

Seven checks before any money moves.

Every new supplier passes the same chain of checks, in order. If one link fails, we stop and tell you why.

  1. Legal entity

    The company is real, registered and matches who you're dealing with.

  2. Payee matching

    Bank details match the verified entity before any payment is made.

  3. Product identity

    The physical product is the one you approved, unit for unit.

  4. Measured specification

    Fill, dimensions and materials measured, not taken on trust.

  5. Regulatory compliance

    Documents checked against your destination market's rules.

  6. Commercial terms

    Price, minimums, incoterm and lead time confirmed in writing.

  7. Fulfilment

    Order flow tested end to end before go-live.

FAQ

Before we begin.

Do you only work with brands switching suppliers?

No. Many brands come to us for a supplier switch, but others start with product investigation, a compliance check, quality testing or own brand development. You can take one service or the full set.

Will you tell us if our current supplier is the right one?

Yes. Your current supplier is measured against the same written brief as everyone else. If they come out on top, or the product is what needs to change, we'll say so.

Which markets do you check compliance for?

We're based in the UK and check ingredients, documentation and labels against the regulations of whichever market you sell into, and give you a written list of any gaps.

Do we have to buy stock in bulk?

Not necessarily. We model dropship against bulk plus warehousing for your volumes and cost both honestly, including when it's better not to commit capital yet.
