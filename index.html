FAQ

Questions brands ask before they switch.

How does Chainex get paid?

We charge a fixed fee for the audit, then a brokerage fee based on the orders we manage. You see every supplier quote, so you always know what you're paying for.

Can I keep some of my current suppliers?

Yes. If a supplier is working well, we keep them and coordinate them alongside the rest of the chain. We only replace links that cost you time or margin.

What size of brand do you work with?

Most of our brands sell between 2,000 and 100,000 units a month online. Pooled ordering is what lets smaller brands reach the minimums usually reserved for larger ones.

Who owns the formulas and supplier relationships?

You do. Formulas, artwork and tooling stay yours, and you can contract with any supplier directly if you ever leave.

Do you handle international shipping and regulations?

Yes. We collect the documents each market needs, including COAs, SDS sheets and product notifications, and work with carriers that ship cosmetics cross-border.
